ACT I — CONCEPT

Rock, Paper & Scissors is a fast-paced, 6-player battle royale built on a predator-prey loop. Three roles, two players each. Every gladiator hunts one role and is hunted by another.

ACT II — WORLD

Designed from initial 2D tactical blueprints to full 3D Unreal Engine blockout, the arena balances competitive sightlines, vertical choke points, and role-based routing for intense 2v2v2 flow.

ACT III — GAMEPLAY

Built on UE5.5's Gameplay Ability System: one character class, role-specific abilities granted at match spawn, and a custom C++ ASC override that fixes Root Motion desync.

ACT IV — IN ACTION

Full multiplayer stack in action: EOS Integration Kit with Epic lobbies, private session codes and real-time voice chat. Some of the trailer's cinematics were made in Sequencer

I served as a Gameplay Programmer, Multiplayer Systems Engineer, Level Designer, and Cinematics Artist for Rock, Paper & Scissors, built in Unreal Engine 5.5. I architected the core ability framework using Unreal's Gameplay Ability System (GAS), implementing Gameplay Abilities, Tags, and Effects on a single modular character class that dynamically grants role-specific abilities upon match spawn.

To solve a critical networking challenge where Root Motion animation states desynchronized ability activation between client prediction and server authority, I developed a custom Ability System Component (ASC) override that tightly synchronizes montage replication with ability commit phases.

For online infrastructure, I integrated the EOS Integration Kit (EIK) paired with the Epic Online Subsystem, building a flexible lobby discovery interface, map filtering, private match codes, and integrated spatial voice chat. In addition, I created the full arena layout from 2D tactical planning to 3D blockout in UE5, optimizing sightlines and vertical traversal for 2v2v2 competitive pacing, and animated cinematic trailer sequences using Sequencer.
